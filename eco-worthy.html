Solar Battery Backup for Home

ECO-WORTHY 48V 314Ah LiFePO4 Battery,Suitable for low voltage off-grid and hybrid home energy storage systems, installed in locations such as garages, workshop, shed，cabins. Composed of 16 A-grade LiFePO4 3.2V 314AH cells, with a cycle count of up to 10,000 (0.5 charge/discharge; >80% SOH); 200A Pace BMS, a professional household energy storage BMS, equipped with a 7-inch color touchscreen and advanced pre-charge circuit with passive balancing; 2*125A (250A) circuit breakers and tripping devices provide double protection for the battery, capable of offering smart protection in case of battery short circuit and BMS failure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Eco-Worthy batteries safe for home use?

+
All Eco-Worthy Batteries Built with premium Grade A LiFePO4 cells, one of the safest lithium technologies available. Built-in BMS protection prevents overcharge, overheating, and short circuits—making it ideal for residential energy storage.

Will it work with my inverter/solar system?

+
Yes. Our batteries are designed to be widely compatible with most 48V inverters and solar systems used in U.S. homes. For best performance, we recommend checking your inverter’s communication protocol or contacting our support team before purchase.

How much power do I actually need for my home?

+
It depends on your usage: 5–10 kWh → essentials (fridge, lights, WiFi); 10–20 kWh → partial home backup; 20 kWh+ → whole-home coverage; Our systems are scalable, so you can start small and expand anytime.

Can I expand the system later?

+
Absolutely. Eco-Worthy batteries support parallel expansion, allowing you to increase capacity as your energy needs grow—without replacing your existing system.

What kind of warranty and support do you offer?

+
We provide up to a 10-year warranty on key battery models, along with U.S.-based support. Our team assists with installation guidance, troubleshooting, and system planning to ensure a smooth experience.
